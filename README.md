## 🎓 Diplomagép – BME VIK slot machine

A standalone browser slot machine themed on university life at the BME informatics faculty (ZH-k, vizsgák, bulik, gólyatábor, puskázás).

- **Nagy díj:** 3× 🏛️ Szigorlat
- **Fődíj:** 3× 🎓 Diploma + the whole growing "Diploma-kassza" jackpot
- 📜 Puska is a wild. Pairs from the left also pay, so wins come often (deliberately easy, with guaranteed Szigorlat/Diploma after a dry streak)
- Lots of animation (spinning reels, suspense on near-misses, coin/confetti/firework particles, screen shake) and fully synthesized sound effects + chiptune music (Web Audio, no asset files)

Play: open `slot-machine/index.html` in a browser. Controls: **Space** = spin, **A** = auto-spin, **M** = mute.
